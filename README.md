# ✿ Pocket Pet ✿

Adopt, name, and care for your own little virtual pet. Vibe coded and designed for mobile first.

🐰 **[Adopt a pet](https://soumyag777.github.io/Virtual-Pet/)**

## Features
- 🐾 **6 pets:** kitty, puppy, bunny, hamster, panda, or chick, each with its own favorite foods
- 🤚 **Pet:** hearts and a happy jump
- 🍖 **Feed:** food drops in, followed by a munch
- 🎾 **Play:** a bouncing ball and excited hops (uses energy!)
- 🌙 **Sleep:** the room turns to night and the pet recharges
- 📊 **Live stats** for fullness, happiness, and energy that go down over time
- 💾 **Saves your pet,** so it's waiting (and missed you) when you come back

## Built with
HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, split into `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js`. Hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Design notes
Time away passes slowly on purpose, so your pet never starves overnight. The game should feel cozy, not guilt-inducing. Color-coded stat bars (mint, yellow, pink) show at a glance what your pet needs.
